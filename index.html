Tame a Forest Hare with berries, a Moon Moth with nectar, or an Ember Drake with grilled fish after defeating Ashwing. Bosses drop powerful weapons and equipment. Equip a glider and hold jump while falling to glide. Submerging your head drains air; you will drown when it runs out. Surface to breathe or craft a Diving Mask, Air Tank, and Air Potions. Platforms can be jumped through from below; hold S to drop through. On touch screens, use the bottom buttons and tap nearby blocks. Chopping a natural tree trunk topples the whole tree and collects its wood. Each biome has unique trees, slimes and ores. Boss arena stone and platforms cannot be mined. Wood pickaxes mine stone; craft a copper pickaxe to mine crystals.

TRAIN WITH THE ELEMENTS

Fighting styles

Visit a style’s home biome and trade materials to learn from its master. Equip a learned style, use F or Attack for close-range elemental strikes, and C or Ability for its special move. Aim with your mouse. Normal styles share a cooldown, even when you change styles. Shadow Fists uses R to enter Eclipse Form for 10 seconds; use the Ride button to mount while this style is equipped. Admin-only Omni Fists has independent C and R abilities. Equip a weapon from Pack to return to sword or bow combat. Admin players can learn every style free.

The Admin

Oh, you found my island! I look familiar, don’t I? These weapons slipped through the cracks in the world. Take one and see what happens.

Each roll begins a 60-second weapon trial. Close this window and try your weapon anywhere. When time runs out, the Admin appears for an unstoppable finale. You respawn at camp and keep the weapon. If you have Oni fists or Admin access, answer “I’m you” to stop the finale and claim every weapon. Further rolls during a trial do not reset the timer.
